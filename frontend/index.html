BEHIND THE PREDICTION

the bigger picture.

A convolutional neural network turns visual patterns into a sports prediction.

01 / PREPARE

A consistent canvas

Your photo is oriented, converted to RGB, and resized to 224 × 224 pixels. The model’s built-in Rescaling layer handles normalization.

02 / RECOGNIZE

A head start in vision

MobileNetV2 transfer learning reuses learned visual features. Your trained classifier connects these patterns to basketball, football, and tennis.

03 / CLASSIFY

More than a label

A softmax layer returns a probability for every sport. The highest score becomes the prediction; the full breakdown stays visible.
